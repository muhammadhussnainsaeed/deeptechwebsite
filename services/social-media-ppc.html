— FAQ

Questions, answered.

What ad budget do I need? ›

A practical starting ad budget for testing and validation in Pakistan typically ranges from PKR 30,000 to PKR 50,000 per month (roughly PKR 1,000 to PKR 1,600 per day). This baseline provides enough data for the algorithms on platforms like Meta or Google Ads to test audiences, keywords, and creative messaging effectively, as spending below this threshold often leaves campaigns stuck in the "learning phase". Once campaigns find product-market fit and begin delivering a positive return on ad spend (ROAS), budgets usually scale into the PKR 100,000 to PKR 300,000+ range to capture broader market share. Crucially, your media spend is paid directly to the advertising platforms with zero markup from us—ensuring you retain full control over your budget and can adjust spend dynamically based on real-time performance. We recommend a budget based on your goals and market.

Which platforms do you cover? ›

We cover the complete modern digital advertising ecosystem, including Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads, YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok. However, we don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach; the channels we deploy depend entirely on where your target audience actively spends their time and the specific nature of your business. For instance, B2B enterprises aiming to generate high-value pipeline typically see the strongest ROI through LinkedIn and intent-driven Google Search, whereas direct-to-consumer and visual brands thrive on the high-engagement, video-first algorithms of Meta and TikTok. We handle the platform-specific strategy, custom creative formatting, and daily campaign optimization natively for each network, ensuring your ad spend is only deployed exactly where it converts best.
